Sales Performance Accelerator

A Training Needs Analysis and blended learning strategy designed to help a fictional B2B sales team close performance gaps and improve conversion outcomes.

SV Solutions60 Sales Representatives3-Month Business Goal
Portfolio simulation: SV Solutions is a fictional company. All interviews, survey findings, metrics, and plans shown here are illustrative; no real-world outcomes are claimed.

01 · The Business Challenge

SV Solutions generates qualified opportunities, but its sales team is not converting enough prospects into customers. The business needs to understand why before choosing interventions.

18%

Current conversion rate

Illustrative baseline
28%

Target conversion rate

A 10-percentage-point increase
3 months

Target timeframe

Proposed business goal, not a result

Problem statement: Investigate the factors contributing to the gap between 18% and 28% conversion, distinguish capability gaps from operational barriers, and recommend measurable interventions.

02 · TNA Approach

A mixed-method analysis triangulates stakeholder perceptions, representative feedback, sales data, and observed behavior.

Stakeholders

  • Sales Director — business expectations
  • Sales Managers — observed performance
  • Sales Representatives — day-to-day barriers
  • Sales Operations — CRM and processes
  • L&D / Instructional Designer — analysis and recommendations

Data collection methods

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • Representative survey
  • CRM pipeline and lost-deal analysis
  • Sales call observations and rubric reviews

04 · Learning Strategy

A four-week blended program combines knowledge acquisition, guided practice, scenario-based decisions, and manager-led reinforcement.

Module 1 · 30 min

Product Knowledge Essentials

Self-paced eLearning with use cases, differentiators, and knowledge checks.

Module 2 · 90 min

Consultative Selling Skills

Facilitated virtual workshop with discovery questions, value propositions, and role-play.

Module 3 · 30 min

Objection Handling

Branching customer conversations, decision points, and targeted feedback.

Module 4 · 30 min

Sales Application & Coaching

Manager-observed practice, skills checklist, feedback, and individual action plan.

Four-week learning journey

Week 1Build product knowledge and establish learning baseline.
Week 2Practice customer discovery and value-based selling.
Week 3Apply objection-handling skills in branching scenarios.
Week 4Complete observed practice and coaching action plans.

Beyond training: Sales Operations should assign ownership of sales materials, establish update cycles, and ensure access to approved resources.

05 · Evaluation & Measurement

Explore the four Kirkpatrick evaluation levels. This is a proposed measurement framework, not evidence of achieved business impact.

Level 1 — Reaction

Use post-training surveys to assess relevance, learner confidence, and perceived usefulness.

Level 2 — Learning

Measure knowledge assessment scores (target ≥85%) and objection-handling rubric performance (target ≥80%).

Level 3 — Behavior

Use manager observations and coaching checklists; target ≥80% meeting the observed skill application rubric.

Level 4 — Results

Monitor CRM conversion rates against the 18% baseline and 28% three-month target, while considering lead quality, pricing, market conditions, and process changes.

Three-month measurement schedule

Month 1

Deliver learning, capture assessments, establish observed behavior baseline.

Month 2

Reinforce through coaching and review early sales trends.

Month 3

Evaluate conversion outcomes and compare against baseline and target.
