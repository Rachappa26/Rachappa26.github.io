Think Before You Prompt

Responsible Use of AI at Work

Watch the Microlearning Video

A short workplace learning experience about using generative AI responsibly and securely.

Project Overview

Learning format: Video-based microlearning

Target audience: Corporate employees

Duration: Approximately 90 seconds

Topic: Responsible use of generative AI

Tools: Microsoft Clipchamp, AI text-to-speech, GitHub Pages

Learning Objectives

After completing this microlearning, learners should be able to:

  • Recognize risks associated with sharing confidential information in AI tools.
  • Identify safe practices for protecting sensitive workplace data.
  • Explain why AI-generated content must be verified before use.
  • Recognize the importance of following organizational AI policies.

Instructional Design Approach

This microlearning uses a workplace scenario to introduce a realistic data privacy risk, followed by three practical behaviors employees can apply immediately.

  • Scenario-based learning: A confidential-data example establishes the relevance of the topic.
  • Microlearning: Concise narration and focused visuals reinforce essential information.
  • Actionable guidance: Three safe AI practices provide clear workplace takeaways.
  • Retention: The closing message, "Pause. Protect. Verify.", reinforces the desired behaviors.
